I am an Associate Research Scientist at [Northeastern University](https://www.khoury.northeastern.edu/), where I work with the [Gyori Lab for Computational Biomedicine](https://gyorilab.github.io/). I build natural-language systems that turn complex scientific and biomedical literature into structured, usable knowledge.

My work sits at the intersection of **NLP, large language models, information retrieval, information extraction, and knowledge grounding**. I enjoy taking research ideas from problem formulation through model and dataset development to tools that help domain experts search, interpret, and act on evidence at scale.

I bring experience leading collaborative, high-impact research efforts. I was a co-PI on [SKEMA](https://ml4ai.github.io/skema/), a DARPA-funded program focused on automating scientific knowledge extraction and modeling; lead AI researcher for [AZHealthTXT](https://azhealthtxt.arizona.edu), which developed text-based tools for healthcare innovation; and a core contributor to [REACH](https://github.com/clulab/reach), an open-source neuro-symbolic system for extracting knowledge from cancer-biology literature.

Previously, at the [University of Arizona](https://www.cs.arizona.edu/), I worked with the [CLU Lab](https://clulab.org) and the [Data Science Institute](https://web.archive.org/web/20251217205311/https://datascience.arizona.edu/). Across academic and industry settings, I have developed machine-learning models and datasets, contributed to open-source NLP systems, built APIs, mentored researchers, and partnered with multidisciplinary teams to solve real-world problems.

I am most interested in building systems that connect retrieval, structured extraction, and grounded reasoning, so scientists and other experts can move from a large, fragmented body of text to answers they can inspect, trace to evidence, and use with to derive new insights.
